Closed-loop protocol

Capture First, Decode Later

High-output days and wipeout days are both real. The mistake is using the same workflow for both.

1. Capture under load

Dump the raw packet with almost zero formatting. Voice note, chat export, or a single markdown dump is enough.

2. Park, do not polish

Polishing under load burns the capacity you need to recover. Parking is the skill.

3. Decode on a high day

When fluency returns, compile: extract levers, decisions, and next actions. That is when agents earn their keep.

Takeaway

Treat mood-state as system state. Capture protocols must survive wipeouts, or the loop leaks.
