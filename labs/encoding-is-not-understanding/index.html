One-line model

Encoding Is Not Understanding

Archives grow. Fluency does not, unless you practice retrieval.

Encoding

You captured the words. The file exists. That is necessary and nowhere near sufficient.

Understanding

You can restate the lever, apply it to a new case, and teach it without opening the note.

Takeaway

Measure understanding by use under load, not by folder size.
