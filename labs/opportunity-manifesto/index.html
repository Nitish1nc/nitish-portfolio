NITISH-LABS EXPERIMENT #01 • NO CORPORATE BUZZWORDS

Stop going around begging for jobs. That’s not how money works.

Most people treat finding work like asking for a favor. Smart folks realize one silly, simple truth: people only give you money when you solve a problem they’re tired of dealing with.

Interactive Reality Check
01 // Wake Up Your Brain

Opportunity is everywhere once you stop thinking in straight lines.

You don't have to hide in a cave just "upskilling" forever, and you don't have to spam 500 strangers with your resume. Both extremes leave massive money on the table.

🧲

Why pick Inbound OR Outbound? Run both.

Posting cool projects online brings people to you (Inbound). Reaching out directly to founders who need help right now gets fast cash (Outbound). Together, they create an unfair advantage.

Rule #1: Never rely on just one door.
🧩

2–3 Big Skills + 7–8 Quick Tricks.

Betting your whole life on "one skill, one portfolio, one job" is fragile. Combine 2–3 core strengths with 7–8 tiny, specific fix-it skills that someone will gladly pay you for this weekend.

Rule #2: Big skills build trust. Tiny skills close deals.
🥞

Stack your income like pancakes.

Why pray to one employer when you can have a steady main gig, two quick freelance retainers on the side, and a small startup idea cooking in the background?

Rule #3: Multiple streams = zero panic.

🔒 NITISH-LABS SECRET VAULT

Wait... How Do You Actually Reach Out Without Sounding Salesy?

Spotting opportunities is only half the game. Inside the lab, we’re testing three unfair plays for getting replies from busy founders. Tap "Peek Clue" on any card—we'll show you just enough to spark ideas.

PLAY #01 98% Open Rate

The 90-Second "Trojan Horse"

Never send a message saying "Hi, are you hiring?" Instead, send something they literally cannot ignore...

Clue Inside:

Record your screen for 90 seconds fixing a tiny broken thing on their website or checkout page. Don't ask for a call—just hand them the fix for free. Watch what they reply within 2 hours...

PLAY #02 1-to-50 Leverage

The "One Person" Backdoor

Messaging 50 founders takes forever. Smart operators message one specific person who drops their name to 50 founders at once...

Clue Inside:

Every startup investor group (like YC or a16z) has a "Talent Helper" whose entire job is helping their 50+ startups find good builders. Impress that ONE person, and you skip every line...

PLAY #03 No More "No"

The 3-Option Menu Trick

When someone asks "How can we work together?", never give them a yes-or-no choice. Hand them a menu instead...

Clue Inside:

Offer Option A: A quick 2-week fix ($X). Option B: A few hours a week on retainer ($Y/mo). Option C: Full-time lead. Even if they can't hire full-time, they almost always pick A or B...
