Hi, I'm Nitish. Most software forces humans to adapt to it. I sit at the intersection of Product Management, Marketing, and Content to ensure we build tools that adapt to the human mind instead.

01. The Philosophy

Beyond features.

The gap between a good product and a great one isn't a longer feature list. It's the elimination of cognitive friction.

Through my work researching and prototyping HCI (Human-Computer Interaction) systems, I've realized that the brain doesn't create in a vacuum. It relies on associative memory, context, and flow. Yet, the tech industry keeps shipping "blank canvas" apps that demand we do all the heavy lifting, causing context-switching fatigue and attention residue.

I bring this perspective into every role I take. Whether I am defining a product roadmap, writing a go-to-market narrative, or designing a user workflow, my core question is always: "How do we make this feel like an extension of thought, rather than a chore?"

I don't just engineer the machine; I figure out why people should care about it, write the story that sells it, and ensure the product delivers on that exact promise.
