Active Capture & export

Vault

Finish the chat. Queue it. Export it. Close the loop. Interpretation later - capture now.

What

Universal Chrome MV3 extension (PRD + Eng Spec V1): modular extractors for major AI sites, generic webpage fallback, IndexedDB queue sidebar, exports to Markdown / JSON / HTML / TXT / ZIP with assets. Capture under ~2s target; never silently discard. Explicitly not a knowledge graph, RAG layer, or auto-tagging brain in V1.

Why

Tabs stay open because fear of losing a good thread is real. Manual copy-paste is the productivity tax nobody budgets for. Vault's litmus test: does this help capture and close the loop - or sneak toward another second-brain product? If the latter, it waits.

Status

Requirements and engineering architecture are locked for V1. Phase 1 Memory Capture MVP (silent Markdown inbox) is the coded screwdriver under NitishLabs active-projects. Full Vault queue UX is the productization path - not inventing more out-of-scope AI features.
