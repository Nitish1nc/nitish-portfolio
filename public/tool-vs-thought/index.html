Interface as instrument

Match the Tool to the Thought

Most AI clients are messaging apps. Some thinking jobs need cards, parks, and forks instead of one scrolling transcript.

Linear chat

One thread. Good for bounded Q and A. Bad when three thoughts want the floor at once.

Spatial desk

Sessions, bubbles, park and pin. You keep the narrative without forcing parallel ideas into a queue.

Takeaway

Stop asking which AI is best. Ask which interface fits the thought you are having.
