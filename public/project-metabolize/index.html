The Problem

Your Notes Are a Cognitive Tax

Traditional note-taking doesn't reduce load. It defers it. Every unprocessed highlight is a debt your future self has to pay.

🔄

Context-Switching Cost

Every time you jump between source and notes, your working memory bleeds. The brain doesn't switch cleanly - it drags residue from the previous task.

Leroy (2009) - Attention Residue
🧠

Working-Memory Overload

Holding key points in your head while you read consumes the same cognitive resources you need for comprehension. Offload too slow, and the loop breaks.

Risk & Gilbert (2016) - Cognitive Offloading
📦

Archive Bloat

Most note systems are built to accumulate. The result: thousands of half-processed artifacts that create background cognitive noise, not clarity.

Kirsh (1995) - External Representation
