A universal Chrome extension: finish a conversation on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok, Perplexity, or Cursor, queue it, and export Markdown, JSON, HTML, or ZIP. Capture first, always: no AI summaries, no auto-tagging, no second-brain ambitions in V1. PRD and Engineering Spec V1 are done.
