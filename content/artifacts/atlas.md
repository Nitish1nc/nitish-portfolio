A spatial thinking workspace you can play in the browser: Session, Workspace, Objects, Messages. Atlas rejects the single scrolling transcript. Conversation Mode keeps structured branches, Workspace Mode is a Figma-like canvas of objects inside a Session. Dual modes, park and pin, compression, and demo replies without an API key.
